# Review Rubric Template

## Major Comments

- Is the objective clear?
- Is the knowledge gap motivated by limitations or challenges rather than absence of prior work?
- Are claims supported by evidence?
- Are methods detailed enough to reproduce or evaluate?
- Are modeling assumptions stated and justified?
- Does the data analysis answer a hypothesis?
- Do figures support the argument?
- Are physical mechanisms explained?

## Specific Suggestions

- Which paragraph, figure, table, slide, or code section should change?
- What should be added, removed, reorganized, or verified?
- What evidence or comparison would strengthen the claim?
