# Student-Facing Disclaimer Template

This assistant provides first-pass feedback based on documented research mentoring guidance and approved examples. It is intended to help you improve your work before sharing it with a mentor, collaborator, reviewer, or committee.

It does not replace human advising, peer review, authorship discussions, submission approval, safety review, legal review, or institutional policy.

Do not upload confidential, controlled, sponsor-sensitive, patent-sensitive, or student-identifiable material unless you are using an approved secure environment and have permission to do so.

Use the feedback as a revision aid. Bring unresolved research-direction questions, authorship questions, major technical decisions, and submission decisions to the human mentor.
