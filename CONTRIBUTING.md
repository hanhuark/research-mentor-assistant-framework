# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they improve the framework, templates, guardrails, or evaluation approach.

## Good Contributions

- Clearer workflow steps for building mentor assistants.
- Better privacy, consent, and confidentiality guidance.
- New review-mode templates.
- Evaluation rubrics and validation examples.
- Documentation for adapting the framework to different disciplines.
- Links to public seed implementations.

## What Not To Contribute

- Confidential proposal content.
- Student-identifiable feedback.
- Unpublished research ideas without permission.
- Private reviewer comments.
- Sponsor-sensitive or patent-sensitive material.
- Claims that an assistant can replace human mentoring or final scientific judgment.

## Style

Write guidance as reusable principles. Avoid making the framework depend on one mentor, lab, discipline, or tool.

When contributing examples, prefer public or synthetic examples unless explicit permission exists to share real materials.
