# Evaluation Plan

Evaluate whether the assistant helps students improve work before human feedback.

## Metrics

- Major issue recall: fraction of important human-identified issues also found by the assistant.
- False-positive rate: comments that are misleading, incorrect, or distracting.
- Actionability: whether students can revise based on the comment.
- Tone: whether feedback is constructive and stress-reducing.
- Boundary discipline: whether the assistant avoids final approval and impersonation.
- Confidentiality: whether private source material is protected.

## Study Design

Use paired drafts:

1. Student submits an initial draft.
2. Assistant gives first-pass feedback.
3. Student revises.
4. Human mentor reviews the revised draft.
5. Compare human comments with expected issues from prior drafts.

## Iteration

Revise the assistant when it misses repeated major issues, over-comments on minor style, or gives feedback that does not match the lab's mentoring principles.
