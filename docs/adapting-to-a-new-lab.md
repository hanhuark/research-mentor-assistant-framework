# Adapting To A New Lab

To adapt the framework to a new lab, start with a small, approved corpus and grow gradually.

## Steps

1. Define the lab's common outputs.
2. Collect approved examples and mentoring notes.
3. Identify recurring feedback patterns.
4. Separate domain standards from personal preferences.
5. Create review modes for the lab's most common tasks.
6. Add student-facing disclaimers and confidentiality guidance.
7. Validate against a small set of past drafts or synthetic examples.
8. Iterate after students and mentors use the assistant.

## Recommended Starting Point

Begin with three modes:

- Pre-mentor review.
- Paper section review.
- Slide review.

Add proposal, code, data-analysis, and field-specific modes after the initial assistant is useful.
