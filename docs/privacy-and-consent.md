# Privacy And Consent

A mentor assistant should be built only from materials that are approved for the intended use.

## Public-Safe Materials

- Published papers.
- Public talks.
- Public datasets and code.
- Public course materials.
- Publicly documented rubrics.

## Restricted Materials

- Student drafts.
- Private proposal documents.
- Pending manuscripts.
- Sponsor materials.
- Reviewer comments.
- Patent-sensitive disclosures.
- Internal meeting notes.

Restricted materials may still inform private calibration when permission, access control, and institutional policy allow it. Public repositories should contain generalized principles, not confidential examples.

## Consent Checklist

- Are the source owners aware of the intended use?
- Is student-identifiable information removed?
- Are unpublished ideas protected?
- Are sponsor and IP constraints respected?
- Is the deployment environment appropriate for the data?
