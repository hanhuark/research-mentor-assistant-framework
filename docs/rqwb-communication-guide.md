# Research-Quality Writing Benchmark Communication Guide

## Recommended Terminology

Use **Research-Quality Writing Benchmark (RQWB)** as the formal name. It states the object being evaluated without implying that AI use is inherently improper or that authorship can be inferred from prose.

Supporting phrases:

- Reader-centered technical writing
- Evidence-and-mechanism writing
- AI-assisted research-writing quality
- Source-grounded technical revision

Avoid claims that a workflow makes writing "human," "undetectable," or proven not to use AI. The benchmark measures communication quality, not authorship.

## LinkedIn Post Draft

AI-assisted writing does not become better by replacing a few suspicious words.

The harder problem is that an AI draft can be grammatically correct yet scientifically thin, repetitive, overqualified, or difficult for a real reader to follow. I have been assembling a six-skill workflow for **research-quality writing**: writing that helps readers understand the problem, evidence, mechanism, limitation, and conclusion.

1. [Academic Research Skills](https://github.com/Imbad0202/academic-research-skills) provides the broad research-to-publication workflow.
2. [Mechanical Engineering Research](https://github.com/hanhuark/mechanical-engineering-research-skill/tree/main/skills/mechanical-engineering-research) adds domain judgment: physics, assumptions, validity ranges, methods, and uncertainty.
3. [Research Writing and Literature](https://github.com/hanhuark/mechanical-engineering-research-skill/tree/main/skills/research-writing-literature) focuses on narrative logic, literature synthesis, equation explanation, and figure discussion.
4. [Reviewer-Author Loop](https://github.com/hanhuark/reviewer-author-loop-skill) supports critique, revision, verification, and re-review.
5. [Avoid AI Writing](https://github.com/conorbronsdon/avoid-ai-writing) flags patterns for contextual review without treating them as proof of authorship.
6. [Clarity](https://github.com/addyosmani/clarity) asks the most important question: will a real reader understand the point?

For example, this sentence only lists equation inputs:

> A theoretical force-balance formula predicting departure diameter from contact angle, surface tension, and fluid/vapor density difference was used.

A stronger explanation names the physics:

> The correlation estimates bubble departure diameter by balancing buoyancy against the vertical component of surface tension at the contact line. The density difference determines buoyancy, while the contact angle affects the surface-tension component that holds the bubble to the surface.

The first version describes variables. The second describes the mechanism.

The goal is not to hide AI use. It is to preserve author responsibility and make technical writing more specific, evidence-based, and useful to readers.

I am developing the [Research-Quality Writing Benchmark](research-quality-writing-benchmark.md) to compare these complementary workflows through source-grounded cases and blinded technical ratings. The current [capability report](rqwb-capability-report/README.md) documents intended scope only; it does not claim a performance advantage before the outcome study is complete.

## LinkedIn Article Outline

Suggested title: **From Fluent to Useful: Evaluating AI-Assisted Technical Writing**

1. Explain why grammatical fluency does not guarantee scientific usefulness.
2. Separate authorship detection from research-writing quality.
3. Introduce the six complementary roles and the capability report.
4. Show one annotated before-and-after example with the source boundary and mechanism explanation.
5. Describe RQWB case design, blinded scoring, and privacy controls.
6. Report only collected outcome evidence, including rater disagreement and limitations.
7. Invite public or synthetic cases and independent raters after rights review.

## Publication Guardrails

- Do not quote restricted proposals, manuscripts, student work, reviewer reports, or sponsor material.
- Do not report a skill ranking unless matched outputs receive blinded ratings.
- Label documented scope, pilot observations, and empirical results separately.
- Preserve links to every skill so readers can inspect its actual instructions and scope.
