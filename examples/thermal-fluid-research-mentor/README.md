# Thermal-Fluid Research Mentor Seed

This seed implementation shows how the Research Mentor Assistant Framework can be applied to a thermal-fluid mechanical engineering research context.

The implementation is maintained separately as the [Thermal-Fluid Research Workflow](https://github.com/hanhuark/mechanical-engineering-research-skill).

## What It Demonstrates

- Technical writing guidance for clear paragraph logic.
- Literature review as critical synthesis rather than paper-by-paper summary.
- Professional citation grouping and knowledge-gap framing.
- Detailed experimental and modeling methods.
- Baseline-case and hypothesis-driven data-analysis planning.
- Four-level figure discussion: description, observation, physical explanation, and comparison with prior work.
- Graphics-focused slide and poster guidance.
- Proposal-development guidance.
- Research coding and reproducibility expectations.

## How To Adapt It

Use this seed as a pattern, not as a universal standard. Replace the domain examples, mentor guidance, rubrics, and review modes with materials from the target research group or discipline.
